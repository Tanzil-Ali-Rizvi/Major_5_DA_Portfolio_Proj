# [Meta] Marketing Mix Modeling & Budget Optimization

## 🎯 Business Problem
The objective was to evaluate the effectiveness of a **$2.5M annual marketing budget** across Search, Social, and Podcast channels. Specifically, the goal was to identify which channel provided the highest marginal return on investment (mROI) to guide future spend reallocation.

## 📊 Key Results
- **Podcast Alpha:** Identified that Podcasts deliver a **3.5x marginal ROI**, which is **40% higher** than traditional Search (2.5x).
- **Optimization Strategy:** Recommended shifting 15% of the underperforming Social budget ($2.0x ROI) into Podcasts to capture higher marginal returns.
- **Model Accuracy:** The OLS regression model accounted for over 90% of revenue variance, providing a high-confidence roadmap for budget scaling.

## 🛠️ Methodology
1. **Data Aggregation:** Compiled 52 weeks of spend and revenue data, accounting for varying investment levels across channels.
2. **Regression Modeling:** Built a Multivariate Linear Regression model (MMM) to attribute revenue to specific marketing inputs.
3. **ROI Extraction:** Isolated the coefficients for each channel to determine the "Marginal Return"—the revenue generated by the next dollar spent.
4. **Budget Simulation:** Simulated a reallocation of $375k (15% of total budget) to project total revenue lift.
